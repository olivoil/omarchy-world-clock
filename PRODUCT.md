# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Omarchy users who coordinate across timezones from the desktop bar. They need
to scan current times and convert a reference time without leaving their active
workspace.

## Product Purpose

Provide a world-clock panel that feels like a native Omarchy bar popup while
supporting richer multi-timezone planning than the built-in clock. Success
means the entry point, placement, dismissal, theme, and visual chrome are
indistinguishable from the surrounding shell.

## Brand Personality

Native, precise, quiet.

## Anti-references

- Centered application-window or modal presentation.
- A palette, border, radius, or transparency treatment that diverges from the
  active Omarchy shell theme.
- Decorative card treatment that competes with the time information.
- Marketing copy or onboarding inside the working panel.

## Design Principles

- Behave like part of the shell before expressing an independent identity.
- Make timezone comparison fast to scan and stable while clocks update.
- Use Omarchy's active runtime tokens instead of approximating a theme.
- Keep advanced editing available without adding noise to the read view.
- Preserve useful behavior across Omarchy versions where practical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus and error states, readable contrast,
and stable layouts. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
